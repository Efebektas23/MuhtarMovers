Canada ↔ USA

One move. One coordinated team.

A border adds paperwork, not a second moving company. We plan customs documentation, inventory, transport and delivery as a single sequence.

Pickup in Canada or the US · Coordinated delivery on the other side

  1. 01
    Customs documentation The forms required for a household goods shipment are prepared with the move, not left for the border booth.
  2. 02
    Inventory requirements A usable inventory is built from what you are actually moving — enough for customs, without asking you to catalogue every spoon first.
  3. 03
    Border procedures Timing, inspection windows and what travels with you versus what travels on the truck are planned in advance.
  4. 04
    Transportation The same operation that loads your home is responsible for getting it across the line.
  5. 05
    Delivery coordination Unload date, access and placement are confirmed with you — so arrival is not a surprise.
